## Motivation

The user rejected the v8 registration for `06830` while considering the other
nine Redwood registrations generally acceptable.  A post-hoc diagnostic found
that the nearest of the 24 PCA-axis rotations was still 48.108 degrees from a
GT-backed proper-Sim(3) oracle.  The failure was therefore missing orientation
coverage, not merely local TTT thresholds or scale refinement.

## Method and inference contract

- Frozen input geometry:
  `gpt_version/06830/pixal3d_sampled_100k.ply` and
  `gpt_version/06830/pixal3d.glb`; neither was regenerated.
- Shared, category-agnostic rotation initialization: compose each of the 24
  proper PCA rotations with the fixed Euler grid `{-45, 0, 45}^3`, deduplicate,
  and coarse-rank 648 rotations.
- Coarse scales: `0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.4`.
- Fine-rank 24 candidates using the saved camera, partial silhouette,
  visible-depth consistency, and partial-to-complete surface distance.
- Jointly refine isotropic scale and translation, then locally refine the best
  eight rotations using the same self-supervised objective.
- Ground truth is not loaded by the v10 script and is not used for inference,
  ranking, selection, or parameter tuning.
- No fusion, point deletion, non-rigid deformation, or anisotropic scale.
  All 100,000 frozen Pixal points are preserved.

Implementation:
`scripts/run_pixal_so3_lattice_sim3_ttt_v10.py`, launched through
`run_pixal_so3_lattice_sim3_ttt_v10.py` from the project root.
